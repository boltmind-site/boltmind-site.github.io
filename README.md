# BoltMind

Public site: https://boltmind-site.github.io/

The previous address https://herozero-assistant.github.io/hero-zero-assistant/ is kept as a redirect bridge (repository herozero-assistant/hero-zero-assistant) so old links keep working.

Static product site for Hero Zero automation. The same current program screenshots are shown in all six site languages: twelve desktop images and eleven Android APK images in `assets/screenshots/`. Tabs, captions and platform labels are translated in `platform-copy.js`. The app interfaces shown in the screenshots are in English. The desktop images show the BoltMind 4 panel with fictional example accounts and values; they were rendered offline from a synthetic snapshot, without the game or real accounts.

The page also presents **BoltMind Heroic Sense** as a separate, free, read-only mission advisor. It ranks available missions by XP per energy or coins per energy and does not automate gameplay. Its download is hosted on the site. The BoltMind Bot screenshots and features belong only to the full bot.

Advertised lifetime prices are BRL 100 on Hotmart for Brazil and USD 20 on Payhip for USD purchases, covering PC and Android. The site displays the checkout currency instead of estimated exchange rates. Update the prices separately in Hotmart and Payhip; the site does not control those checkouts. `pricing.json`, `app.js` and `platform-copy.js` carry the displayed values. The coupon promotion has been removed; the purchase section highlights one-time payment, individual access, support, and recurring updates and fixes.

Support is Discord and email. Login and tickets are intentionally deferred at the user's request. No credentials or private customer data are collected by the site.

Earlier history (including the previous design, branch backup/boltmind-before-real-product) stays in the herozero-assistant/hero-zero-assistant repository.
